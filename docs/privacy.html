Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

GoHomeNow ("we", "our", "us") operates the GoHomeNow mobile app, which helps people find dogs to adopt and plan for a successful adoption. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Adoption profile answers — the housing type, work schedule, budget, activity level, experience level, household composition, and city you provide during onboarding, so we can match you with suitable dogs.
  • Account information — if you create an account, your email address and any profile details you choose to add.
  • Usage and device data — basic app usage and diagnostic data (e.g. crash logs, app version, device type) used to keep the app working reliably.
  • Communications — if you contact us for support, we keep the information you send us.

We do not require and do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, or payment card details unless you explicitly provide them (for example, to complete a subscription purchase, which is processed by Apple and not stored by us).

How We Use Information

  • To match you with dogs and shelters that fit your lifestyle and budget.
  • To connect you, at your request, with partner vets, trainers, groomers, or insurers.
  • To improve matching quality and app reliability.
  • To communicate with you about your account or support requests.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With shelters or partners you choose to connect with through the app.
  • With service providers who help us run the app (e.g. hosting, analytics, crash reporting), under obligations to protect your data.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of GoHomeNow or others.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the app's features, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your Choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

GoHomeNow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
